Continuous detection mechanisms are essential for securing AI supply chains that operate at machine speed, since periodic security checks fail against autonomous coding agents. Ohad Shalev proposes three detection strategies: scanning asset locations, classifying binaries as managed/unmanaged/malicious, and establishing continuous monitoring feeds to catch Shadow AI before deployment. The article argues that organizations must implement detection across binaries, source code, build manifests, and agent configurations to prevent unvetted AI assets from entering development pipelines.
