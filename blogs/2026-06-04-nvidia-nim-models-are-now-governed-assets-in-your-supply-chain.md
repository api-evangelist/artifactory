JFrog AI Catalog now governs NVIDIA NIM models as first-class supply chain assets, bringing them under the same controls as Docker images and other software artifacts. Previously, developers and coding agents pulled NIM models directly from NVIDIA's NGC registry, bypassing organizational security policies. The integration enables unified discovery, explicit allow/block governance, and complete audit trails for AI models consumed across the enterprise.
